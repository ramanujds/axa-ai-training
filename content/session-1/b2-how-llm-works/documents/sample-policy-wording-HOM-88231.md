# Sample Home Cover Wording

**Fictional - for training only. Not a real policy.**

**Policy number:** HOM-88231
**Product:** Sample Home Cover
**Wording version:** 1.0

## 1. Cover limit

We pay up to EUR 50,000 per claim.

## 2. Burst pipes

Sudden bursting of water pipes inside the home is covered. Gradual leaks are not covered.

## 3. Excess for water damage

You pay the first EUR 250 of each water damage claim.

## 4. Reporting a claim

Tell us within 14 days of finding the damage.

## 5. What is not covered

- Flooding from rivers or the sea.
- Damage to garden fences.

## 6. Empty homes

If the home is empty for more than 30 days, water damage cover is limited to EUR 10,000.
